# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

A broad, design-conscious audience creating distinctive backgrounds for apps, websites, presentations, and desktop setups. The primary workflow is fast visual exploration: adjust a small set of expressive controls, save promising seeds, and export a production-ready image.

## Product Purpose

Aetheria turns a compact mathematical parameter set into high-resolution, atmospheric contour artwork. Success means a user can discover a compelling composition in seconds, recover or share it exactly, and export it at the resolution they need without specialist graphics software.

## Positioning

A live generative instrument that makes cinematic, mathematically structured backgrounds feel immediate and controllable.

## Brand Personality

Fluid, tactile, atmospheric. The voice is precise and quiet, allowing the generated artwork to remain the dominant experience.

## Anti-references

The supplied visual reference establishes the direction: soft silver atmosphere, saturated cyan masses, and warped halftone transitions. Avoid neon contour-line fields, ornamental sci-fi HUD styling, novelty vaporwave motifs, and controls that compete with the canvas.

## Design Principles

- Keep the artwork edge-to-edge and visually primary.
- Make every control produce an immediate, legible visual response.
- Balance creative surprise with reproducibility through seeds and URLs.
- Reveal complexity progressively instead of presenting a graphics editor.
- Treat export as a first-class creative outcome, not a utility afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Respect reduced-motion preferences, support keyboard operation and visible focus states, provide non-color feedback for state changes, and keep control labels readable against dynamic artwork.
