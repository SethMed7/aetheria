# Community Code of Conduct

Aetheria is an open-source project. Participation should make the project safer, clearer, and more useful for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving specific, constructive feedback
- Respecting different experiences and viewpoints
- Accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes
- Focusing discussion on the project and its users
- Helping newcomers contribute successfully

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing someone else’s private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that does not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/SethMed7). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents Aetheria in public spaces.
